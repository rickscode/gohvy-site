Privacy Policy

LAST UPDATED: 20 JULY 2026

The short version: your training data belongs to you. We store what the app needs to work — your email, your subscription state, your workout history synced to your account — and nothing else. We never sell your data. Deleting your account deletes it from our servers.

Who we are

GOHVY ("we", "us") is a high-intensity training app available on iOS, Android, and the web, operated from the United Kingdom. This policy explains what data we collect, why, and what your rights are. For any privacy question or request, contact support@gohvy.com.

What we collect

  • Account data. Your email address (used to sign in), your GOHVY Pro subscription or trial status, and a daily count of your requests to MILO, the AI coach.
  • Training data. Your workout plans, logged sets, weights, reps, progression targets, and workout history. This lives on your device and is synced to your account so you can sign in on any device and pick up where you left off.
  • AI request metadata. When you use MILO we record technical metadata about each request — timing, token counts, and validation outcome — to keep the service reliable. This metadata does not include your workout content or your question.
  • Purchase data. Subscriptions are processed by Apple or Google and managed through RevenueCat. We receive your subscription status, never your payment details.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, advertising identifiers, or any tracking data. There are no ads and no third-party analytics trackers in the app.

How MILO, the AI coach, uses your data

When you ask MILO a question, the relevant workout evidence (your recent logged sets and computed analysis for the exercises you asked about) is sent with that request so the answer can be grounded in your real data. The request is processed by our AI infrastructure provider to generate the response. Your questions and workout evidence are used only to answer you — they are not used to train AI models, and we do not store your question content on our servers.

How we use your data

  • To operate the app: sign-in, subscription entitlements, syncing your training history across your devices, and answering your MILO questions.
  • To keep the service reliable and prevent abuse (request metadata and daily usage counts).
  • To understand and improve GOHVY using aggregated, de-identified statistics — for example, how often lifts stall across all users, or which tracks are completed most. These statistics can never identify you, and we only ever analyse them in aggregate.

We never sell your personal data, and we never share it with third parties for their marketing.

Where your data lives

Account and synced training data are stored with our backend provider, Supabase, in the European Union. Sign-in emails are delivered via Resend. MILO requests are processed by our AI infrastructure provider at the time of your request. Each provider processes data only on our instructions.

How long we keep it

Your data is kept while your account exists. AI request metadata is kept for a limited period for reliability monitoring. If you delete your account, your account record, subscription entitlement, AI usage records, and synced workout history are permanently deleted from our servers. Workout history stored locally on your device stays on your device until you remove the app.

Your rights

Under UK and EU data protection law (GDPR) you have the right to access, correct, export, restrict, or delete your personal data, and the right to complain to a supervisory authority (in the UK, the ICO).

  • Delete: Settings → Delete Account in the app removes everything from our servers immediately — no email required.
  • Access or export: contact support@gohvy.com and we will provide a copy of your data.

Children

GOHVY is not directed at children and is intended for users aged 16 and over.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes — for example, collecting a new category of data — we will update this page and note it in the app before the change takes effect.
